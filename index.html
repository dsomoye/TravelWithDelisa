About me

Hi, I’m Delisa. Travel isn’t a hobby, it’s how I live.

I took my first solo trip at 13 and I haven’t stopped. Over the last 23 years I’ve sailed around the world…twice! I’ve studied abroad, and I’ve traveled as a solo traveler, a wife, a mom, and a friend who always plans the group trip. I’ve traveled with friends and strangers alike. Buses, trains, planes, ships, road trips through several countries: I’ve traveled by every means possible.

That range is what makes my content different. I backpacked through Europe as a broke college student, and as a working professional I’ve traveled first class and stayed in 5-star hotels. I know what a budget-savvy backpacker needs, what a family of five needs, and what someone celebrating in a five-star suite expects. I tell the truth about all of it, and my audience knows it.

Outside of travel, I’m an engineer, project manager and entrepreneur, with a bachelor’s in chemical engineering from Rice University and a master’s in management from Harvard University. I bring that same discipline to every collaboration: clear communication, organized planning and content delivered the way we agreed.
